About China Guide

One gateway to China's local expertise

China Guide is an international-facing platform connecting visitors with China's destinations, local professionals, wellness experiences, healthcare providers and business resources. We believe that experiencing China should be easier, more accessible and more meaningful. Through local connections, personalized planning and coordinated services, we help international visitors explore China beyond the ordinary travel experience.

Our Mission

To make China more accessible, understandable and meaningful to the world.

Our Vision

To become a trusted gateway connecting international visitors with China's people, places and opportunities.

Our Values

What guides our work

  • Trust
  • Professionalism
  • Local Knowledge
  • Cultural Understanding
  • Meaningful Connections

From the Founder

A Personal Connection to China.

Behind China Guide is a passion for connecting people, cultures and opportunities. With more than a decade of experience in the travel industry and international cross-cultural exposure, our founder believes that meaningful travel goes beyond visiting famous landmarks — it is about understanding people, discovering authentic experiences and building connections that last.

Today, China Guide brings this philosophy to life by helping international visitors experience a more accessible and meaningful China.

Yiyong Liu — Founder, China Guide
