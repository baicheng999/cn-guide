Local Guide Network

A connected network of local expertise

Every city in China has its own character, culture and stories. China Guide connects international visitors with local guides and service professionals who bring these destinations to life. Our goal is to build a connected network of local expertise, making it easier for international visitors to discover China with confidence.

Cities We Serve

Local Guide Profiles

We coordinate local guides and interpreters across major languages, matching you with the right language support for your trip.

04

Local Experiences

From city highlights to off-the-beaten-path local life, our network helps you experience China through the people who live there.

05

Professional Service Partners

We work with selected local service partners — transport, accommodation and on-the-ground coordination — to support your journey.

We only present guides and service professionals with confirmed cooperation, real existence and necessary credential verification.
