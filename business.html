How It Works

Our business visit process

From your first inquiry to on-the-ground support, here is how we coordinate a business visit in China.

1

Tell Us Your Needs

Share your industry, products of interest and objectives.

2

Industry Classification

We map your interests to the right clusters and regions.

3

Factory Screening

We shortlist suitable factories and suppliers for your request.

4

Factory Confirmation

Each factory visit is confirmed with the supplier before it is arranged.

5

Itinerary Design

We build a day-by-day business itinerary with meetings and logistics.

6

Interpretation & Accompaniment

Bilingual interpreters join every meeting and factory floor visit.

7

Transport & Accommodation

We arrange hotels, transfers and daily transport end to end.

Factory visits and supplier meetings are arranged only with the supplier's prior confirmation. China Guide provides coordination, interpretation and logistics — we do not represent any supplier and we do not guarantee factory access, transactions or commercial results.
