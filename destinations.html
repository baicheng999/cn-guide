Destinations Across China

Fifteen destinations, each with its own character, culture and local experts. Ask us about any city in China — we will explore suitable local options for you.

01

International Gateway

Trade, culture and cuisine. The natural entry point for overseas visitors to South China.

02

Shenzhen 深圳

Technology & Innovation

Tech, innovation and electronics. The innovation engine of the Greater Bay Area.

03

Dongguan 东莞

Manufacturing Edge

Factory tours and manufacturing. Our deepest local resource advantage.

04

Shanghai 上海

Global Metropolis

Metropolis, business and culture. China's most international destination.

05

Beijing 北京

Classic Destination

History, culture and the Great Wall. The timeless icon for first-time visitors.

06

Chengdu 成都

Leisure & Culture

Pandas, food and laid-back culture. A signature slow-travel experience.

07

Chongqing 重庆

Mountain City

Mountain skyline, night views and hotpot. A differentiated, energetic experience.

08

Xi'an 西安

Cultural Depth

History and the Terracotta Army. The heart of China's ancient civilization.

09

Guilin 桂林

Natural Beauty

Karst mountains and rivers. China's most celebrated natural landscape.

10

Kunming 昆明

Yunnan Gateway

Yunnan and ethnic culture. The gateway to China's southwest diversity.

11

Tibet · Lhasa 西藏·拉萨

Highland & Faith

The roof of the world — Potala Palace, monasteries and sacred highland landscapes.

12

Xinjiang · Urumqi 新疆·乌鲁木齐

Frontier & Culture

Vast deserts, Silk Road heritage and vibrant ethnic cultures across China's west.

13

Harbin 哈尔滨

Ice & Snow Capital

China's winter wonderland — the Ice and Snow Festival and Russian heritage.

14

Zhangjiajie 张家界

Natural Wonder

Sandstone peaks and pristine forests — the real-life inspiration behind the floating mountains of Avatar.

15

Sanya 三亚

Tropical Resort

Crystal beaches and tropical islands — China's premier winter escape by the South China Sea.
