01

Payments in China

Mobile payments, cash and international cards — what to sort out before you land.

Learn More Show less
  • Mobile payment is part of daily life in China; check with your bank and app providers before departure what will work for you.
  • Keep a small amount of cash for situations where a digital payment is not convenient.
  • Tell us how you prefer to pay and we will include setup guidance in your arrival plan.
02

Getting Around China

High-speed rail, airport transfers, taxis and city transport — the options you will meet on the ground.

Learn More Show less
  • High-speed rail links many major cities; check current routes and schedules for your travel dates.
  • Airport transfers, taxis and city transport can be planned in advance around your itinerary.
  • Leave room in the schedule for station procedures and local traffic between connections.
03

Internet & Connectivity

SIM cards, staying connected and the everyday apps that make getting around easier.

Learn More Show less
  • Decide before departure how you will stay connected — a local SIM, roaming or another option that suits your device.
  • The services and apps you use at home may work differently in China; check the ones you rely on in advance.
  • Our local contacts can help you sort out the essentials once you arrive.
04

Food & Local Culture

Dining customs, everyday habits and the small social cues that help conversations go smoothly.

Learn More Show less
  • Meals are often shared, and a host may serve guests as a sign of welcome.
  • Regional cuisines vary widely — share any dietary needs or restrictions before your trip.
  • Small courtesies at the table, such as letting elders start first, are widely appreciated.
05

Healthcare & Medical Visits

What a hospital visit typically involves — registration, appointments, interpretation and having someone with you.

Learn More Show less
  • Bring your passport, insurance details and any medical records you may need for a visit.
  • Appointments and language support are arranged case by case, subject to provider availability and eligibility.
  • Share your requirements early so the options can be discussed with the relevant providers.
06

Business Travel in China

Preparing for site visits, factory tours, meeting etiquette and professional interpretation.

Learn More Show less
  • Clarify the purpose of your visit, the sites you hope to see and who you expect to meet before fixing travel dates.
  • Plan meeting materials, business cards and interpretation needs ahead of time.
  • Allow time between meeting locations for travel and follow-up discussions.
