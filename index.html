A Personal Connection to China.

Behind China Guide is a passion for connecting people, cultures and opportunities. With more than a decade of experience in the travel industry and international cross-cultural exposure, our founder believes that meaningful travel goes beyond visiting famous landmarks. It is about understanding people, discovering authentic experiences and building connections that last.

My childhood followed the water. I was born beside Poyang Lake in Jiangxi and did my primary and middle school in Quanzhou, Fujian — the Minnan dialect still sits on my tongue. Later I returned to Poyang and studied tourism management at Hainan College of Economics and Business. In 2010 I earned my national guide license. I had no idea then how far that piece of paper would take me.

For the sixteen years that followed, I was almost always on the road. I started as a ground guide in Hainan, Tibet and Nha Trang, Vietnam. I spent six years in the Philippines, building a small hotel brick by brick on Panglao Island — and later sold it, for a new road. The last two years were in Japan, walking travelers from around the world through day trips beyond Tokyo. Today I live in Guangzhou. I am a certified national guide and have hosted guests from more than sixty countries and regions.

What I do now is simple: take the roads I have walked in China, Japan and Southeast Asia, write them into journeys that belong to you, and hand them to guides who truly know that land — to walk it with you, step by step.

Yiyong Liu
Founder, China Guide

Questions, answered

The things overseas travelers ask us most before they book.

What is China Guide?

China Guide is a platform connecting international visitors with local travel, wellness, healthcare and business service resources across China.

Which countries do you serve?

We welcome international visitors, with a particular focus on Southeast Asia, including Malaysia, Singapore, the Philippines, Vietnam, Indonesia and Thailand.

Can I customize my China itinerary?

Yes. We can help coordinate travel arrangements based on your destinations, interests, schedule and requirements.

Can you arrange medical appointments in China?

We can assist with appointment coordination and related travel arrangements through appropriate healthcare providers, subject to availability and eligibility.

Can you arrange factory visits?

Yes. We can coordinate inquiries and factory visits based on your industry interests and the availability and approval of relevant factories.

Do you provide English-speaking guides?

We can help arrange language support and local guide services, subject to destination and availability.

How do I make a booking?

Contact our team with your requirements. We will review your request, confirm service availability and provide a proposal.

How do I make payments?

Payment methods, deposit requirements and payment terms will be provided in the service proposal and confirmed booking agreement.

Is China Guide a travel agency?

Travel services are delivered by a licensed inbound travel agency, Shanghai Kaixuan Sightseeing Travel Service Co., Ltd. China Guide coordinates planning, local connections and support; the contracting and licensed service delivery are handled by the agency.
