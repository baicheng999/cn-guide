From the founder

Beyond the postcard, meet the real China.

My childhood followed the water. I was born beside Poyang Lake in Jiangxi and did my primary and middle school in Quanzhou, Fujian — the Minnan dialect still sits on my tongue. Later I returned to Poyang and studied tourism management at Hainan College of Economics and Business. In 2010 I earned my national guide license. I had no idea then how far that piece of paper would take me.

For the sixteen years that followed, I was almost always on the road. I started as a ground guide in Hainan, Tibet and Nha Trang, Vietnam. I spent six years in the Philippines, building a small hotel brick by brick on Panglao Island — and later sold it, for a new road. The last two years were in Japan, walking travelers from around the world through day trips beyond Tokyo. Today I live in Guangzhou. I am a certified national guide and have hosted guests from more than sixty countries and regions.

What I do now is simple: take the roads I have walked in China, Japan and Southeast Asia, write them into journeys that belong to you, and hand them to guides who truly know that land — to walk it with you, step by step.

Plan slowly, with someone who has walked there.

Sixteen years across China, Japan and Southeast Asia. Tell me when you are coming and what you dream of seeing — I will write it into a route first; then, on the ground, a local guide will walk every inch of it with you.

My promise is a simple one.

The same care I gave guests in Tokyo, Panglao and Lhasa goes into your journey in China, exactly as it did then.

Questions, answered

The things overseas travelers ask us most before they book.

Do you help with Chinese visas?

We send you a tailored itinerary and hotel list you can use for your visa application. If your nationality requires an invitation letter, tell us your dates and cities and we'll advise what's needed — final issuance is always decided by the embassy.

How do I pay?

Trips are contracted and invoiced by our licensed agency, Shanghai Kaixuan Sightseeing Travel Service Co., Ltd. We confirm available methods such as bank transfer, major cards and common international options in your written plan before any payment — no money is taken up front without a signed plan.

Will my guide speak English?

Yes. Every private guide in our network is English-screened, and many also speak a second language. Tell us your language needs and we match accordingly.

Can I combine cities or change the plan?

Always. Every trip is tailor-made. Mix cities, add rest days, swap activities — your plan is built around your interests, dates and pace, not a fixed package.

Is it a private tour or a group tour?

Private, by default. You travel with your own guide at your own pace — small groups of family or friends, never a mixed bus tour.

Is this a legitimate and safe service?

Yes. All trips are delivered by a licensed inbound travel agency, Shanghai Kaixuan Sightseeing Travel Service Co., Ltd., with verified, license-checked local guides. Your contract and payments go through the agency.

What if my plans change?

We keep terms fair and clear. Tell us as early as you can; changes and cancellations follow the written policy in your plan, and we help rebook where possible.
