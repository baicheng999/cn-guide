How It Works

Our medical coordination process

From first inquiry to follow-up, here is how we coordinate a wellness or medical visit in China.

1

Submit Your Needs

Overseas clients submit their wellness or medical interests through our inquiry form.

2

Initial Consultation

We discuss your goals, timeline, group and preferences in a first conversation.

3

Match a Medical Institution

We identify suitable licensed hospitals or clinics based on your needs.

4

Institution Review & Confirmation

The medical institution reviews the request and confirms eligibility and availability.

5

Appointment Coordination

We arrange appointment times, documents and logistics with the provider.

6

Translation & Accompaniment

A bilingual coordinator accompanies you and translates every consultation.

7

Accommodation & Transport

We arrange hotels, transfers and daily transport so the trip stays simple.

8

Follow-up Support

After your visit we help with reports, follow-ups and any further coordination.

Medical services are delivered by licensed hospitals and clinics in China. China Guide provides coordination, appointment assistance, translation and travel support only — we do not provide medical diagnosis, treatment or prescriptions, and we do not guarantee any medical outcome. We never ask for detailed medical records, ID numbers or sensitive health data through our website forms.
